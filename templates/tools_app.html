what we build for you

The toolbox below is free and runs in your browser. These are the custom builds we do for clients -- scoped to your business, running on infrastructure you own or that we host, yours to keep. Tell us what's eating your time and we'll tell you what we'd build.

content autopilot

Write a post once; it publishes to your site and schedules out to Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Google Business from one place.

  • one dashboard to draft and approve
  • per-platform formatting, scheduled
  • optional AI drafting in your voice, approved by you

lead engine

Warm leads pulled from public data before competitors reach them -- permits, new registrations, licenses, filings, contract awards.

  • filter by location, industry, size, timing
  • enriched with public contact info
  • delivered daily/weekly or into your CRM

review & reputation monitor

Tracks what people say about you on Google, Yelp, Facebook and industry sites.

  • instant alerts on new (and negative) reviews
  • suggested responses for your approval
  • monthly sentiment summary

competitor watch

Keeps an eye on competitors without spending your evenings on it.

  • pricing, products, hiring, ads, socials
  • weekly digest of what changed and why it matters

exposure audit

Shows what an attacker can learn about your business and staff from public sources. Run only on systems you own, with written permission.

  • leaked creds, exposed info, email/DNS misconfig, stale software
  • plain-English report with a prioritized fix list
  • optional recurring monitoring

internal ops dashboard

Your key numbers on one screen, pulled from the tools you already use -- Square, Shopify, QuickBooks, Analytics, your CRM.

  • sales, traffic, leads, inventory at a glance
  • automated weekly report to owners

intake & booking automation

Replaces back-and-forth email with forms that do the work.

  • smart forms route, book and confirm
  • reminders and follow-ups on unanswered quotes

custom integrations

Connect two systems that don't talk. Web orders create invoices; new leads post to your team chat; form submissions land in a sheet.

lite CMS

Edit your own site without a developer or WordPress -- a minimal admin panel around your actual content.

  • edit text/images in place, preview before publish
  • role logins, version history, one-click rollback
  • static-first: fast, cheap to host, small attack surface

lite EMS

Team info, schedules and paperwork in one place instead of spreadsheets and group texts.

  • directory, shift scheduling with swaps and reminders
  • time clock with payroll-ready timesheets
  • time-off approvals, doc storage with expiry alerts

Want one of these for your business? get in touch — or see what we've built in the open.
