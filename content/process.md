Every engagement runs the same way, so you always know what happens next.

## 1. tell us what's eating your time

[Send a message](/contact/) about your business and the work you'd
rather not be doing. We reply within two business days.

## 2. we find the leak

We look at how the business actually runs today -- booking, follow-ups,
leads, the admin -- and tell you in plain language where we think we
can help, and where we can't.

## 3. we build the fix

Tools, automations, lead lists, or branding, built around the way you
already work instead of forcing a new system on you.

## 4. you get the keys

You own what we build outright. No lock-in, no hostage software, no
subscription to us just to keep it running.
