Two projects, two very different rooms -- one solo and electronic, one
a band that mostly improvised. Recordings and video below; more going
up as it gets finished.

## treeclmbr

treeclmbr was the first thing I ever put out -- a solo beat/electronic
project built on an MPC, a controller, and a laptop running Ableton.
Signed to **Philosophy Major Records**, it lived on the open-mic and
house-show circuit: Philosophy Major Presents nights, Community Dr,
whatever room had power and a PA. Sample-chopped and improvised live
more often than not -- closer to a live set than a record.

A proper writeup (and a video) is coming. For now the recordings are up
on [soundcloud.com/treeclmbr](https://soundcloud.com/treeclmbr):
